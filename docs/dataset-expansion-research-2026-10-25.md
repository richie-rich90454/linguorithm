# Dataset expansion research

Checked 25 October 2026 against dataset publishers' own pages, repositories, and license files. The project now pins EWT plus seven additional English UD treebanks and an OANC written-text context corpus; only their manifests and a provenance summary are committed, while the corpus bytes and caches stay inside the project and out of Git. The current English UD inventory has 13 active treebanks; this release uses eight and excludes five non-commercial or source-text-incomplete candidates. See the pinned [EWT manifest](../training/datasets/ewt-manifest.json), [supplemental UD manifest](../training/datasets/ud-english-manifest.json), [OANC manifest](../training/datasets/oanc-manifest.json), and [training guide](../training/README.md). The corpus split and rights limits documented below remain in force.

## Other grammar/error resources checked

**BEA-2019 learner corpora remain unsuitable for a commercial release under their published terms.** The task site states that W&I+LOCNESS, FCE, Lang-8, and NUCLE are limited to non-commercial use; NUCLE and Lang-8 also require an access request. CoNLL-2014's NUCLE training release requires a signed license form. These are valuable research/evaluation sets, but download availability does not authorize commercial distribution. ([BEA-2019 data and terms](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/nl/bea2019st/), [CoNLL-2014 access page](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~nlp/conll14st.html))

**CoLA is a useful acceptability benchmark, not a correction corpus with clear redistribution rights.** Its official page provides 9,594 public train/dev sentences from 23 linguistics publications and binary acceptability labels, but no corpus license is stated there. The project's baseline repository says the source text's copyright remains with original authors/publishers and only expresses an expectation of US fair use. Do not package or train a commercial release from its sentences without a rights basis; its labels also provide no correction spans. ([official CoLA page](https://nyu-mll.github.io/CoLA/), [official baseline repository terms](https://github.com/nyu-mll/CoLA-baselines))

**cLang-8 is expressly non-commercial.** The Google Research dataset repository says it is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and requires the original Lang-8 source sentences separately, so it is not a commercial release candidate. ([publisher repository](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/clang8))

**C4_200M is a synthetic option with a source-text condition.** The corruption-edit files are CC BY 4.0, but the publisher's instructions require separately obtaining C4 sentences and applying those edits to reconstruct source/correction pairs. The C4 catalog describes C4 as cleaned Common Crawl text. The edit-file license alone therefore does not establish that reconstructed pairs have a standalone redistribution grant; review the base-text provenance and terms before using pairs in a shipping training pipeline. These are synthetic corruptions, not learner-authored errors. ([C4_200M repository and license](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/C4_200M-synthetic-dataset-for-grammatical-error-correction), [TensorFlow Datasets C4 catalog](https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/c4))

## Practical recommendation

The initial license-reviewed expansion is now in place: Atis, CHILDES, and ESLSpok add 40,862 training sentences (286,564 tokens) from three treebanks; CTeTex, LittlePrince, PUD, and Pronouns remain held-out evaluation sets. EWT's official train/dev/test partition is unchanged. OANC contributes 6,424 written documents to contextual correction ranking, with approximate sentence boundaries and no gold syntax or error labels. These sources substantially increase varied text and syntax, but they do not make the model 100 times larger or establish 100 independent datasets. The official English UD inventory and licensing leave only a small number of usable annotated English corpora.

For natural error labels, NICT JLE remains the most promising checked addition, subject to a privacy/tag audit and a separate evaluation showing transfer from speech to writing. UD English ESLSpok is sampled from that source and must share original interview/speaker groups with it if both are used. Keep EWT's official test untouched, and do not treat any of these corpus results as independent learner-essay evidence.

As with EWT, a corpus license does not by itself settle all questions about distributing derived model weights. Keep provenance and the selected model distribution terms explicit in the model card, and preserve each dataset's required notices. The project already documents this distinction in its [provenance notes](research-data-models.md).
