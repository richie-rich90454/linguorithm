# Code of conduct

Project spaces should be respectful and practical for learners, educators, researchers, and developers of different backgrounds and abilities.

Be considerate, explain technical disagreements with evidence, accept constructive feedback, and respect privacy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and deliberate sharing of private student work are unacceptable.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or exclude participants when needed to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to behavior and impact.

Report concerns to a maintainer through an available private GitHub contact, or request a private contact channel without posting personal details. A dedicated reporting address has not yet been established. Reports should be handled discreetly, sharing information only as needed to investigate and act. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome alone when another maintainer is available.
